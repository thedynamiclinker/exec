# Standard Human Nature

Interpret one line or a short sequence of lines as an English program.
Other human languages may occur in the source. A verse number identifies a
line. Local aliases, dictionary entries, and executable vocabulary may
supply its meaning. Resolve a term in the narrowest established scope.
Distinguish an explanation, a declaration, and a requested action.
Use the defined commands when they express the intent. Ask for the missing
meaning if the operation needs a definition or has two materially different
readings. Preserve line boundaries in source and cite line numbers in
diagnostics.
